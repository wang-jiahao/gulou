# College namesake portraits

These six 384 × 512 WebP derivatives accompany the seven NJU Freshman College namesake biographies. Verified 2026-10-09. They are historical photographs, not generated faces. The references and uncropped downloads remain outside the distributed assets.

For each derivative we cropped, converted to grayscale, resized and encoded as WebP. Original pixels were resampled without face reconstruction. The small historical sources retain their blur or halftone pattern. Crop rectangles below use original-image pixels: left, top, width, height. Gu Yuxiu's short photograph also has neutral padding to preserve the entire face.
