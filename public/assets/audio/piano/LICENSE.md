# Piano sample attribution

Piano recordings from **Versilian Studios / VSCO 2 Community Edition**, edited and distributed in **Nicholas Brosowsky's tonejs-instruments** library; packaged as **tonejs-instrument-piano-mp3 1.1.2** by **Makefully Studios**.

- Sample library: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
- Source information: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/master/sample-source-info.txt
- Package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/tonejs-instrument-piano-mp3/v/1.1.2
- Samples license: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**, as specified by the library README: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Original instrument collection: http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html

Changes for Gulou: selected 16 pitches, capped at eight seconds with a final 350 ms fade when applicable, resampled to 32 kHz mono and encoded as 80 kbit/s MP3. At runtime notes are transposed, softly filtered and mixed with generated room reverb. These sample recordings remain under CC BY 3.0; the project's MIT license does not replace their license. No endorsement is implied.

The Gulou composition and sampler implementation are original project code. No Minecraft recordings or melodies are included.
