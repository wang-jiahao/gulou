Reconstructed geometry and building dimensions are approximate; no endorsement by the university is implied.

Piano recordings are from Versilian Studios / VSCO 2 via Nicholas Brosowsky’s tonejs-instruments, packaged by Makefully Studios; samples are **CC BY 3.0**, with source links and processing details in [piano attribution](public/assets/audio/piano/LICENSE.md). See docs/asset_sources.md for the asset inventory and public/credits.html for the in-game credits.

These are approximate derived silhouettes, not original vector calligraphy. Source photos remain excluded; no separate copyright claim or university endorsement is implied.
